## Follow-up visits
A follow-up visit with the same doctor within 14 days of the first consultation is charged at 50% of the consultation fee.

## Laboratory tests
The in-house lab offers blood tests including complete blood count (CBC), fasting blood sugar, lipid profile, thyroid profile (TSH), liver function and kidney function tests. Most results are ready within 24 hours. A lipid profile costs PKR 1,800 and a CBC costs PKR 900.

## Payment
The clinic accepts cash, debit and credit cards, and bank transfer. Patients with corporate health insurance from partner companies should bring their insurance card and CNIC.
