# Appointment Policy

*Fictional sample data for demonstration purposes.*

## Booking
New patients must register with their full name, mobile phone number and date of birth before booking. Each appointment slot is 30 minutes long.

## Arrival
Please arrive 15 minutes before your appointment time to complete check-in. Bring your CNIC and any previous medical reports or prescriptions.

## Late arrival
If you are more than 15 minutes late, your appointment may be rescheduled to the next available slot.

## Cancellation and rescheduling
Appointments can be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge up to 4 hours before the appointment time by calling reception. Repeated missed appointments without notice may require an advance payment for future bookings.

## Children
Patients under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.
