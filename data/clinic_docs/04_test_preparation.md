# Test Preparation Guide

*Fictional sample data for demonstration purposes.*

## Fasting blood tests
For a fasting blood sugar test or a lipid profile, do not eat or drink anything except plain water for 10 to 12 hours before the test. Morning samples between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM are recommended.

## Medicines before tests
Continue your regular medicines unless your doctor has told you otherwise. Bring a list of all medicines you take.

## Thyroid test (TSH)
No fasting is needed for a thyroid test. If you take thyroid medicine, ask your doctor whether to take it before or after the sample.

## Results
Results are shared by SMS and can be collected from reception. Your doctor will explain the results at your follow-up visit. The assistant cannot interpret test results.
